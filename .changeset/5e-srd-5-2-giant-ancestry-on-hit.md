Fire's Burn, Frost's Chill and Hill's Tumble are offered on a weapon or Unarmed Strike hit: the Goliath's hit option adds 1d10 Fire or 1d6 Cold to the hit's damage roll (doubled on a Critical Hit), and `changes` effects on the attack's damage step (now tagged `attack-hit`) slow or knock Prone the target (Large or smaller) and spend the Giant Ancestry use. The powers' own actions stay for other attack rolls. Conformance suite: `5e52-attack-frosts-chill`, `5e52-attack-hills-tumble-too-large`.
