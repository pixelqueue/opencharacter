A magic item's power that is used since dawn or out of charges isn't offered: each `use: item-power` that presets `daily` or `charges` (the elemental-commanding bowl, brazier and censer, Dagger of Venom, Dragon Scale Mail, Dust of Dryness, Javelin of Lightning) has `available` on the use. Conformance suite: `5e52-javelin-of-lightning-used`.
