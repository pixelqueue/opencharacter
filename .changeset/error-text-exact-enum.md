Error text for equal bounds and enum values: `errors.out-of-range.exact` words an `out-of-range` whose bounds are equal (`"Effort" must be exactly 1.`), and `errors.wrong-type.values` a `wrong-type` for an enum, whose data now carries the enum's `values`. `text.error` names each value by its label (a parameter's from its action's text, a field's from the field's) and joins them as alternatives (`"For" must be The scene or The day.`).
