Use the new format features: Death Ward, Oil, Hypnotic Pattern and Eyebite's sleep end themselves; Multiattacks run a charge (`charged`) and the Half-Dragon's Draconic damage; Eldritch Spear and Improved Elemental Fury extend the cantrip's range; sibling picks are read with `picksOf` (the `origins.backgroundPlusTwo`, `origins.cantripReplaced`, `warlock.pactWeapons` and `warlock.lessons` fields are gone); the smite slot level and Lesser Restoration's condition are choice steps over values; backgrounds preset Magic Initiate's list (its `list` state is gone); species traits and Gift of the Depths keep their own uses (moved off the species and Warlock copies).
