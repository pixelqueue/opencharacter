Less clutter on every sheet. Spell slot ranks, the extra slot and its spells show only when the character has those slots, and the divine font only for a font. A skill's proficiency rank is its modifier's companion, so the Skill proficiency group is gone, and a Lore shows only once trained. A custom Lore's subject input sits in Skills, as "Custom Lore subject (1)". Damage immunities, a weapon parry and Wave Fan activations show when they apply; the remaining helper values are hidden.
