Class-only display fields show only for their class: bard muse, champion and cleric sanctification, cause, doctrine, druidic order, mystery, bloodline, patron, arcane school and thesis, and Drain Bonded Item's uses. The elemental influence shows for the elemental bloodline, the evolution spells with Arcane and Occult Evolution, favored weapons with a favored rank, the familiar's weapons when it has some, and alchemical bombs when above martial weapons. Swim, fly, climb and burrow speeds show when the character has them. The cleric's and champion's sanctification are "Deity's sanctification" and "Champion's sanctification".
