Deflect Attacks reduces the damage directly: a `deflectAttacks` situational choice on an incoming attack reduces its Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing damage (any type with Deflect Energy) by 1d10 plus the Dexterity modifier and Monk level, never below 0. The `deflect-attacks` action stays for redirecting the force.
