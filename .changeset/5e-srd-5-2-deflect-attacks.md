Deflect Attacks reduces the damage directly: a `deflectAttacks` situational choice on an incoming attack reduces the damage change it is selected on, if Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing (any type with Deflect Energy), by 1d10 plus the Dexterity modifier and Monk level, never below 0. A situational choice reaches only its own change, so an app selects it on one damage change of the attack (README, Limitations). The `deflect-attacks` action stays for redirecting the force.
