Upgrade steps `removeState`, `moveState` (a copy's state to a pick or to granted content's saved state) and `removeChoice`; `renameState` also renames picks' and grants' saved state. `renameState`, `removeState` and `moveState` take `item`, limiting the step to one item's copies, picks and grants (with `type`, both must match). A choice step's `owner` can be a path to a choice nested in an inline option (`classes.operative/specialization/ghost`). `checkRelease` accepts the new steps as coverage: `removeState` and `moveState` for `state-removed` (with `item`, only that item's key), `removeChoice` for `choice-removed`, and steps naming nested owners for nested choices. It no longer reports written `values` replaced by a formula as removed options.
