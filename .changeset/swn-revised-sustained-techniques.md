`sustain-technique` is offered only on the techniques that last while their Effort stays Committed (a new `sustained` field: Cloak Powers, Concert of Minds, Kinetic Transversal, Metamorph, Neural Trap, Pressure Field (its shield for comrades), Prophecy, Spatial Awareness, Stutterjump, Telekinetic Armory and Telekinetic Flight), no longer on every technique. Apps that start `sustain-technique` on any other technique now get `unknown-action`; they commit its Effort with `commit-effort` (scene or day) instead.
