Cunning Strike, Stunning Strike, Open Hand Technique, Hurl Through Hell and the Rod of Lordly Might's Drain Life and Paralyze are parameters of each attack (`cunningStrike`, `cunningStrike2`, `stunningStrike`, `openHand`, `hurlThroughHell`, `drainLife`, `paralyze`). On the hit, an `action` effect on the attack's `attack-hit` step hands the target its saving throw at the feature's DC and applies the condition (or Hurl Through Hell's 8d10 Psychic damage) on a failure; the cost (a Focus Point, the use or a Pact Magic slot, the rod's daily use) is spent with it. `weapon-attack` and `unarmed-strike` are tagged `attack-hit` (and the Unarmed Strike `unarmed`), so the parameters are added to those attacks only, Open Hand Technique's to the Unarmed Strike. Cunning Strike's dice come off the Sneak Attack, read from the parameters; an effect the rogue can't use (the new `rogue.strikeEffects`: not known yet, or Poison without a Poisoner's Kit), or effects together costing more than the Sneak Attack, do nothing and cost nothing. Its DC is the new `rogue.strikeDc` field. `item-power`'s outcome data adds `failed`. Breaking: the `cunning-strike`, `stunning-strike`, `open-hand-technique` and `hurl-through-hell` actions, the rod's `drain-life` and `paralyze` actions, and the `cunningStrike` situational choice are removed. A paused action saved under 0.3.0 that runs one of the removed or changed actions can't be resumed: start it again.
