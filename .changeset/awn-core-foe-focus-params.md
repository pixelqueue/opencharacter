Apex Predator, Robot Whisperer and Zombie Smasher add their attack bonuses through `attack` parameters (`beast`, `robot`, `zombie`): half the level to damage and Shock against beasts, +4 to hit and damage against robots, and at Zombie Smasher's level 2 a roll that counts as a natural 20 and 8 more damage against zombies.
