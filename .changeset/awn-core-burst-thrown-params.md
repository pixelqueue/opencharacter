`attack` offers `burst` only on burst weapons and a new `thrown` parameter only on melee weapons that can be thrown, in place of the `burst` situational choice. A thrown attack is ranged and deals no Shock. Armsmaster and Deadeye now both reach thrown attacks, and only the better of the two counts there (the `thrown-focus` stack key, spanning hit and damage).
