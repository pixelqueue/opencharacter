Retaliation is modeled, as in 5.1: when the Berserker takes damage and stays up, each equipped melee weapon offers its attack, and an Unarmed Strike is offered too, as follow-ups (`barbarian.retaliation`). Whether the damage came from within 5 feet, and the Reaction, are the player's.
