Psychic Succor Commits Effort for the day on its first use in a scene, as the book says, and is free for the rest of the scene (a new `scene.succor` input, cleared by `end-scene` and `rest`) or always with Mastered Succor. Its healing at the biopsion's level shows on the technique as computed values (`healDice`, `healBonus`), which the roll reads.
