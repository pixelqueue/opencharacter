The attacks' damage steps are tagged `attack-hit`. Stunning Strike and Open Hand Technique are parameters of each attack (`stunningStrike`, `openHand`): on the hit, an `action` effect on that step spends the ki and hands the target its saving throw at the ki save DC, stunned or knocked prone on a failure. Breaking: the `stunning-strike` and `open-hand-technique` actions, which only reported the DC, are removed.
