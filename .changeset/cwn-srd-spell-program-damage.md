Spells that deal damage per level (Arson, Blast, Blastwave, Disrupt, Paralysis, Slow, Stun, Stunwave) compute it at the caster's level, and the Kill and Stun Verbs at the hacker's Program level (`damage`, such as `3d6`); Skin of Steel computes its Armor Class (`ac`). Their descriptions show the value on a held copy (`{self_damage}`, `{self_ac}`).
