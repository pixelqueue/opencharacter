Use the new format features. Additional Lore is one value choice whose effects reach the picked Lore's fields (saved picks keep their values). Fixation grants Additional Lore with its own Lore choice and presets Assurance from it, so its `topic` choice is gone: the upgrade moves a saved topic to the granted Additional Lore. Harmonic Sensitivity asks whether it trains Performance or gives Assurance in it, recommending what the rules pick; the upgrade asks saved characters. Elemental weapon and elemental barrier are applied to the caster with their element (Cast a Spell's new `element`), and while one is active the elemental connection's wall of fire and absolute zero take its element's trait and damage type. Guidance, sure strike (which now gives its advantage), true target and Treat Poison or Treat Disease end on the roll they change.
