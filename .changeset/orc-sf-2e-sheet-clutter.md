Less clutter on every sheet. Spell slot ranks, the extra slot and its spells show only when the character has those slots. A skill's proficiency rank is its modifier's companion, so the Skill proficiency group is gone. A custom Lore's subject input sits in Skills, as "Custom Lore subject (1)". Gun, solar, agile melee and familiar weapon proficiencies, damage immunities, a weapon parry and implanted augmentations show in their groups when they apply; the remaining helper values are hidden.
