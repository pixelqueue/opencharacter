Content is listed in content order, the load order then each pack's content order, instead of by qualified ID: the ruleset's `content`, query matches, choice and choice step content options and random draws over them. A dependency's content comes first, so an automatic run's `options[0]` is the core pack's whatever an add-on pack is called, and a character's homebrew comes last.
