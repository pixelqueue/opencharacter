Mutation categories are content of the new type `mutationCategory`, and a mutation's `category` names one (`mutation-categories.sense`) instead of an enum value, so an add-on pack's category joins the "two in one category" pick; the d6 table keeps the book's six. The stigma fields are open to add-on packs' values. Steps add-on packs' rules effects reach carry tags: `creation.kit`, `creation.mutation`, `creation.finish` and `advancement.level`.
