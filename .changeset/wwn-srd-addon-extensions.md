A spell's `list` and an art's `tradition` are open to add-on packs' values. The arts and Effort checks and `arts.owed` read the `artTrad` and `effortTrad` families instead of naming each tradition, and the traditions whose arts armor stops are the new families `armorTrad` and `heavyArmorTrad`. Steps add-on packs' rules effects reach carry tags: `creation.purse`, `creation.finish`, `advancement.level`, `rest.night` and `rest.scene`.
