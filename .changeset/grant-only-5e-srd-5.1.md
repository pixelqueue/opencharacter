Grant-only content (#172): class features, subclasses, ability score improvements, fighting styles, hunter tactics, metamagic, invocations, pact boons, racial and monster traits, subraces, draconic ancestries, background features, feats and monster spellcasting are held only through their grants and picks; `addItem` of one is `not-addable`, and apps leave them out of add-item catalogs.
