`gain-edge` finishes `closed` for the 5th-level Edge below 5th level and for the Underdog Edge without the rule. `attributes.modTotal` sums the modifiers of the rolled scores, so the Underdog bonus Edge can't undo its own eligibility and every random start finishes. `end-scene`, `rest`, `first-aid`, `reload`, `use-drug`, `gain-attribute`, `trigger-cyber`, `acquire-wired-cyber`, `acquire-focus-gear` and `summon-spirit` end with a declared result. Martial Instinct's counters show only for characters who have it, and the committed Effort tiles have short labels.
