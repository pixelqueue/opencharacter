Use the new format features: Death Ward holds its target at 1 hit point and ends; Eldritch Spear keeps a 300-foot range template on eldritch blast (the `eldritchBlast.range` field is gone); Arcane Recovery and Natural Recovery pick spent slots in a choice step instead of `level1`..`level5` params; the once-per-rest invocations keep `used` on their own pick, Relentless Endurance and Infernal Legacy keep their uses on the trait (no longer on the class or race copy); the dragonborn breath weapon is once per short or long rest; creation and `create-monster` pick in the copy they added.
