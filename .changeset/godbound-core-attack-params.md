`attack` drops its manual `maximize` parameter: Unerring Blade and Surge of Strength add their own parameter to the attacks they fit, committing the scene's Effort (Unerring Blade also hits on anything but a natural 1), and the weapon's `equipped` and a held gift's Effort gate their `use` entries. `gift-blast`'s `mob` parameter is offered only on gifts with Mob damage.
