Divine Smite is offered on the hit, as 5.2's is: a melee weapon attack's on-hit choice offers a smite per spell slot level the paladin has left (and the pact slot); picking one spends the slot and adds 2d8 radiant plus 1d8 per slot level above 1st (5d8 at most), plus 1d8 against an undead or a fiend, to the hit's damage roll, so a critical hit doubles it. The `divine-smite` action stays. Conformance suite: `5e-paladin-sacred-weapon-attack` expects the on-hit choice, `none` recommended.
