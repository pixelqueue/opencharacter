A project names what it teaches by reference, `learnsRitual` (a ritual) or `learnsDesign` (a design), in place of the free-text `learns`, so a custom project's lesson is checked at load and a form can offer the items. The creation starts' descriptions and the `creation-dots` text match the steps: a special ability (or a homebrew playbook's), a vice, three starting action dots (a playbook's own instead), then four more.
