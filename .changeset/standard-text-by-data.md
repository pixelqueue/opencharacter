Standard text by an error's data: `errors.out-of-range.min` and `errors.out-of-range.max` for an `out-of-range` with only one bound, and `errors.safety-limit-exceeded.<limit>` for each safety limit. `text.error` and `text.issue` use them, and show `errors.other` instead of the raw template when a message misses an argument. The `safety-limit-exceeded` and `out-of-range` descriptions in `error-codes.json` say what their data holds.
